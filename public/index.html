To chat completely offline without any Wi-Fi router:

  1. Turn on Bluetooth on this computer and your mobile phone.
  2. Pair both devices in Bluetooth settings.
  3. On the phone or PC, enable Bluetooth Tethering (Personal Area Network).
  4. Open the assigned Bluetooth IP in the phone's browser.

No Wi-Fi router available?

1. Simply turn on your phone's Mobile Hotspot (you can keep Mobile Data turned OFF!).

2. Connect this PC/laptop to that hotspot.

3. Scan the QR code or visit the IP displayed above on any connected phone or laptop!

4. All devices can now exchange unlimited messages, large files, and voice notes at maximum wireless speed!
